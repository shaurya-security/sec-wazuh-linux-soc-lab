ENDPOINT INVESTIGATION
----------------------
The Linux endpoint was examined after the detection sequence.

Observed state:

1. ssm-user was a member of the sudo group.

2. Root's crontab contained:

   */5 * * * * /bin/echo SOC_LAB_PERSISTENCE_TEST >> /tmp/soc-lab.log

3. The root cron spool existed with expected root ownership
   and restrictive permissions.

4. /tmp/soc-lab.log contained eight persistence-marker entries.

5. The persistence marker had executed successfully under the
   root cron job.

6. The targeted persistence search identified the root cron
   spool as the relevant persistence mechanism.

Recovery assessment decision:

   REBUILD

Conclusion:

Root-level persistence was confirmed on the compromised endpoint.
The endpoint was therefore treated as untrusted.


EVIDENCE PRESERVATION
---------------------
Wazuh alerts, endpoint recovery-assessment output, screenshots,

The compromised endpoint was retained temporarily for evidence
